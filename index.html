Before you present

  • Preserve the supplied layout, aspect ratio, and official logo treatment.
  • Use the approved colours and typefaces in the brand guide; keep text legible from a distance.
  • Give each slide one clear takeaway. Use visuals to support your message, not to crowd it.
  • Check names, dates, data, and permission to use photos before sharing.

THE BRAND SYSTEM / 02

Designed to belong.

Use these foundations to keep every post recognisably Toastmasters.

A

Clear hierarchy

Lead with one strong headline, then make dates, names, and details easy to scan.

◧

Consistent colour

Let Loyal Blue and True Maroon carry the design; use accents with intention.

◌

Respect the mark

Use official logo assets without recolouring, stretching, or placing them on busy imagery.

WORDMARK SPECIFICATIONS / 03

Give the mark room.

Use the official Toastmasters wordmark asset in a form that stays clear and legible.

OFFICIAL VARIANTS
Full-colourBlackWhite

Choose the supplied version that has clear contrast with its background. Obtain the asset from the official Brand Portal; do not recreate its lettering.

Go to Brand Portal ↗
CLEAR SPACEX

Leave at least one wordmark-height of empty space around every side. Keep text and imagery outside that area.

MINIMUM SIZE72px

Minimum width on the web. For print, use at least ¾ inch wide.

Never recolour, stretch, distort, place on a busy background, or overlap the official mark. See pages 9–12 of the current Brand Manual for the full examples and logo lockups.

COLOUR GUIDELINES / 04

The palette.

Tap a swatch to copy its HEX code. Primary colours should lead; accents support them.

White and black can support contrast. The Brand Manual also specifies limited gradients; consult it before adding a gradient to a design.

TYPOGRAPHY GUIDELINES / 05

Words with presence.

Keep the type hierarchy consistent from headline to fine print.

01 / HEADLINES & SUBHEADS
Speak
boldly.

Gotham or Gotham Condensed

Primary typeface. Montserrat is the approved free alternative.

GothamGotham CondensedMontserrat
02 / BODY COPY & CAPTIONS
Ideas become stronger when every voice has room to be heard.

Myriad Pro

Secondary typeface; Condensed, SemiCondensed, and SemiExtended are listed variants. Source Sans 3 is the free alternative.

Myriad ProSource Sans 3
03 / SYSTEM FALLBACKS

Arial and Segoe UI are tertiary options for body copy.

04 / SPECIAL EVENTS

Corinthia and Luxurious Script may be used selectively for occasions such as a club anniversary.

Use standard type styles. Avoid word art, heavy effects, and altered letterforms. Refer to the official Brand Manual for full usage guidance.
